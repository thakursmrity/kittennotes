About us

Where Curiosity Meets Calm

Kitten Notes started with a simple belief: personal growth should feel playful, not rigid. So we design stationery that makes self-discovery fun and meaningful.

Our inspiration comes from two places. The endless curiosity of kittens, who poke at everything just to see what happens. And the quiet calm of a cozy journaling session with a cup of something warm.

Today we make guided journals and planners, tear-off notepads, bookmarks and creative prompts. Each one is made to help you set goals, dream, reflect and, most of all, make a little time for yourself.

Thanks for being here. Now go fill some pages.

With purrs, the Kitten Notes team

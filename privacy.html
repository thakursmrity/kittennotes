Privacy policy

This policy explains what information Kitten Notes collects and how we use it.

What we collect

When you place an order, contact us or sign up for updates, we receive the details you share: your name, email, phone number, shipping address and order details, plus any messages you send us.

How we use it

We use your information to process and ship orders, handle returns, reply to your messages and, if you sign up, send you updates and offers. You can unsubscribe from updates at any time.

Sharing

We share only what is needed to fulfil your order, for example your name, phone and address with our courier partner, and with payment providers to process payment. We do not sell your personal information.

Storage

This website does not use tracking or advertising cookies. Your cart is saved only in your own browser so it is still there when you come back.

Your choices

You can ask us to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you by writing to info@kittennotes.com.

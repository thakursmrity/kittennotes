Refund policy

Returns

You can request a return within 7 days of receiving your item. Items must be unused, in the condition you received them, in their original packaging, with proof of purchase.

How to start a return

Email us at info@kittennotes.com. If your return is accepted, we send you return shipping instructions. Items sent back without an accepted return request cannot be accepted.

Damaged or wrong items

Please check your order when it arrives and contact us right away if anything is damaged, defective or not what you ordered, so we can make it right.

Exchanges

The quickest way to get something different is to return the original item and, once the return is accepted, place a new order.

Refunds

We let you know once we have received and inspected your return. Approved refunds go back to your original payment method within 10 business days. Your bank may take a little longer to show it. If more than 15 business days have passed since approval, please contact us.
